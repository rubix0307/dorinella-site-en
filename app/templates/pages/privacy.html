Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain words, what {{ site.DOMAIN }} knows about you, why, who else sees it and what you can do about it. It covers the website, your account and your purchases.

What we collect

Just visiting. When you open a page we record the page, the day and time, the site you came from (its name only), campaign tags in the link (utm_source and the like), your device type (phone, tablet, desktop) and a daily fingerprint made of your IP address and browser details. The fingerprint is a one-way hash that changes every day, so we cannot read an IP address back from it. We use these numbers to see which pages help people. Bots and our own admin pages are not counted. If you click a link that leaves the site, we also record where it went and the browser identification string.

Signing in. You sign in with a Google account; there are no passwords. Google tells us your e-mail address and the name on the account. We do not see your Google password, contacts, mail or files.

Tests and the course. When you take a self-test (for example the Bristol stool scale test) we store your answers and the result so you can see them again. The course keeps what you answer in it (for example the safety check and your goal) and which pages you finished. These answers are linked to your random visitor ID, and to your account once you sign in. Some of them describe your digestion or health. We use them only to show you your result and to run the course; please do not type anything you would not want stored. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Buying. We store your order: the number, the product, the amount, the time, its status, the version of the legal texts you accepted at checkout and the time you accepted them. Payment is made on a page that Stripe hosts. Your card number never reaches our servers, and we never store it. Stripe sends you the receipt by e-mail.

Writing to us. If you e-mail us, we keep the message and our reply so we can help you.

Errors. If something breaks, an error report with the page address and technical details of the request (which can include your IP address) may be sent to our error-monitoring service.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To give you what you asked for (your account, your purchases, your test results, your access): needed to carry out our agreement with you.
  • To run and improve the site (visit statistics, preventing abuse, fixing errors): our legitimate interest in a working, safe site, using as little personal data as we can.
  • To meet legal duties (keeping accounting records of sales, answering authorities): legal obligation.
  • Where you give it (for example sensitive answers you choose to enter in a test or the course): your consent, which you can withdraw by asking us to delete the answers.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not take decisions about you by automated means that have legal effect on you.

Your rights

Wherever you live you can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold about you. Write to {{ legal.seller.email }} from the address of your account; we may ask you to confirm it is you. We answer within one month (45 days in California).

If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland you also have the right to receive your data in a portable form, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before), and to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to delete it, to correct it, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated worse for using these rights. In the last 12 months we collected identifiers (e-mail address, name, visitor ID, IP-derived hash), commercial information (your orders), internet activity (pages viewed, clicks) and answers you gave in tests and the course, for the purposes above. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. An authorized agent may write to us on your behalf.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 16, and you must be 18 (or the age of majority where you live) to buy. If you think a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to the site are encrypted, sign-in is handled by Google, payments by Stripe, and access to our database is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and if the change is important we will tell you by e-mail or on the site. Earlier versions are available on request.
